# Cretan Hieroglyphic Open Corpus

**0.5.0 — catalogue spine, sign registry, provenance and validation foundation**

An open, provenance-first research corpus for Cretan Hieroglyphic designed to preserve scholarly disagreement, distinguish observation from interpretation, and support reproducible comparison with Linear A, Cypro-Minoan and Linear B.

## Research family
- `hawkinsnick/Linear-A`
- `hawkinsnick/Cypro-Minoan`
- `hawkinsnick/Linear-B`
- this repository: native Cretan Hieroglyphic corpus

Repositories remain independent while sharing an Aegean Epigraphy Interchange layer.

## 0.5.0 corpus status
- complete 331-entry CHIC 1996 catalogue spine;
- H 001–122, I 123–179, S 180–315, Y 316–331 series structure;
- 96-entry numbered core-sign registry with no imposed phonetic values;
- object, surface, occurrence, transcription, sign and crosswalk data models;
- source registry, provenance policy, controlled vocabularies and validation gates;
- explicit coverage statistics separating catalogue presence from source-checked transcription;
- cross-script correspondences remain attributable claims, never native sign identity.

The catalogue spine is deliberately conservative. Presence means the CHIC catalogue identifier is represented; it does not mean detailed text, archaeological metadata, museum inventory, segmentation or readings have yet been independently verified here.

Primary reference: J.-P. Olivier and L. Godart, with J.-C. Poursat, *Corpus Hieroglyphicarum Inscriptionum Cretae*, Études Crétoises 31 (1996).
