# Critical corpus contract — 5.0

Version 5 establishes the production contract for inscription-level evidence. A critical corpus is not a single preferred transcription. It is a set of attributable observations/readings, exact locators, review events, disagreement relations and derived views.

Every source-checked reading requires an exact source locator. Diplomatic reading precedes normalization. Restoration is never silently inserted into diplomatic text. Competing readings coexist. Graph identity and sign identity remain separable. Post-CHIC material retains extension identity until a documented concordance exists.

No record may be promoted merely to improve coverage statistics.
