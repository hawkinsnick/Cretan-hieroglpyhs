# Corpus and editorial model

Six separable layers are mandatory: **object**, **surface**, **sign occurrence**, **transcription**, **assertion**, and **interop**. No reading is required to create an object or occurrence. Segmentation may be uncertain or competing. Cross-script similarity is a claim, not an identifier merge.

Canonical objects use `CHIC-###`. External catalogue designations live in crosswalks. Surfaces and occurrences receive locally stable child IDs.

## Evidence rules
Unknown is data: missing values remain null/absent and are never reconstructed merely to satisfy a schema. Preserve source readings before normalization; distinguish diplomatic, normalized and secondary transcriptions; attribute disagreements; record uncertainty; never infer phonetic values from graphic similarity alone.

## Provenance
Every source-derived field or assertion must cite a source ID. Corrections are additive and attributable. Superseded readings should remain recoverable.

## Rights
Do not commit third-party scans, photographs, facsimiles or extensive source transcriptions without redistribution rights. Bibliographic references may point to lawful access locations.
