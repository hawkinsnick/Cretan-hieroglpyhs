# Roadmap

## 0.1 — architecture
Ethics, rights, provenance, source lineage, native schemas, interchange and experiment gates.

## 0.2 — source/catalogue registry
Register major catalogues, editions, databases and museum/object identifiers with rights and lineage.

## 0.3 — inscription/object registry
Create stable project-local identities without treating one catalogue as unquestioned truth.

## 0.4 — occurrence layer
Encode source-supported sign occurrences, loci, uncertainty and direction/layout evidence.

## 0.5 — competing transcription layer
Represent multiple transcriptions/segmentations without silent reconciliation.

## 0.6 — archaeological and palaeographic context
Populate support, site/find context, chronology, hands and sign-instance evidence where sourced.

## 0.7 — structural statistics
Run script-internal recurrence, positional, entropy, graph and clustering analyses under declared null models.

## 0.8 — Linear A comparative framework
Apply identical script-neutral algorithms independently to CH and Linear A. Cross-script sign correspondences remain a separate hypothesis layer.

## 0.9 — hostile audit
Audit coverage, lineage independence, rights, segmentation sensitivity, uncertainty, null models and cross-script leakage.

## 1.0 — research-ready comparative corpus
Freeze a reproducible CH corpus suitable for responsible structural comparison while preserving native uncertainty and scholarly disagreement.
