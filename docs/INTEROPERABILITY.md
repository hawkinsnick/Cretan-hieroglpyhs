# Aegean interoperability contract

Cretan Hieroglyphic remains a native corpus first. Interoperability is a projection layer.

## Shared concepts
Stable record identity, external identifiers, assertions with provenance, rights metadata, uncertainty, and explicit extensions are suitable for interchange with Linear A, Cypro-Minoan and Linear B.

## Forbidden shortcuts
A proposed graphic resemblance must not merge sign IDs. A proposed phonetic equivalence must not populate a native CH phonetic field without a separately sourced CH assertion. A shared sequence or administrative pattern is evidence for a claim, not proof of linguistic identity.

## Versioning
The repository retains the Aegean Epigraphy Interchange v0.1 schema for compatibility. Native 1.0 schemas may be richer than the interchange representation. Loss during projection must be documented rather than silently discarded.
