# Source access and rights policy — 5.0

The source registry distinguishes evidential usefulness from redistribution permission. A source may be citable and usable for factual verification while its images or substantial transcription remain non-redistributable.

For each major source the project records access status, evidential role, independence class and redistribution status. When rights are unclear, commit bibliographic metadata, project-created structured facts and lawful references rather than scans/facsimiles or substantial copied edition text.
