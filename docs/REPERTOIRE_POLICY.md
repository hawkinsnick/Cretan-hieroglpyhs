# Repertoire policy

CHIC is the standard reference point, but its sign list is also an object of scholarship. The project maintains named layers: `CHIC1996` for published CHIC classification; `POSTCHIC` for individually sourced later proposals; and `PROJECT` for derived analytical groupings that are never presented as consensus.

The numbered 001–096 series must not be described as the complete CH repertoire. CHIC also distinguishes logographic, fractional, numerical and punctuation notation. Candidate signs and motifs remain representable without forcing binary resolution.
