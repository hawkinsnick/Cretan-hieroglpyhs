# Source criticism and scholarly independence

A source count is not an independence count. Publications may reproduce CHIC readings, drawings or earlier editions. Source lineage records whether evidence is independent epigraphic observation, partially independent reassessment, shared upstream evidence or unknown.

Primary witnesses, images/drawings, editions, catalogues, museum records and analytical papers serve different evidential roles. Later corrections and reassessments do not silently rewrite the historical CHIC layer. Every consequential claim should answer: who proposed it, from what evidence, where, and with what degree of independence?
