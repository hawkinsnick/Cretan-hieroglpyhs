# Cross-project conformance

Cretan Hieroglyphic is the fourth independent adapter in the Aegean research family.

Shared: provenance, assertions, uncertainty, rights, source lineage, physical loci, representations, hypotheses, experiment gates and correction history.

Not shared: sign inventories, catalogue identifiers, segmentation conventions, palaeographic typologies, chronology systems, language hypotheses or statistical outcomes.

Cross-script sign relationships must be typed assertions with source/proponent, locator, uncertainty and relation type. They are non-transitive by default. A proposed CH↔Linear A relationship does not propagate into Cypro-Minoan or Linear B and does not imply shared sound or meaning.

A generic abstraction graduates toward a future shared core only after multiple script projects require it without changing its semantics.
