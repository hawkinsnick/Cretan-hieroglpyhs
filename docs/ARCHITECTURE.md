# Architecture — 0.1.0

## Evidence
Objects/documents, identifiers, physical loci, sign occurrences, source-supported structural units, archaeological context and uncertainty.

## Witnesses
Source-specific assertions, source lineage, competing transcriptions/readings and explicit disagreements.

## Research
Sign/correspondence hypotheses, frozen analytical specifications, experiment gates, claims, results and errata.

## Interchange
Aegean Epigraphy Interchange v0.1 provides only script-neutral records/assertions/provenance/uncertainty/rights/lineage/loci/representations/hypotheses.

## Clients
Future search, Python, CLI, visualization and export clients consume interchange/native adapters and cannot redefine evidence.

## Dependency rule
Interpretations may depend on observations. Observations may not depend on interpretations.

## CH-specific guardrails
No Linear A AB identifier becomes a Cretan Hieroglyphic sign identity by resemblance. No Linear A source-word semantics are imported. Catalogue/sign labels remain source-attributed until project identities are explicitly modeled.
