# Rights policy

Project-authored software is MIT-licensed unless otherwise stated. The MIT license does **not** purport to license third-party scholarly corpus content.

Modern editions, transcriptions, classifications, drawings, photographs, database compilations and annotations may carry separate rights. Third-party scholarly data retain upstream attribution and applicable terms. Ancient inscriptions themselves are distinct from modern scholarly representations.

Do not commit protected material unless redistribution rights are documented. Where rights are unclear, store source identity, bibliographic locator, factual metadata where permissible and references rather than copying protected material. Transformation or normalization does not erase upstream rights.
